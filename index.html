Privacy Policy

Water Sort: Alchemy Lab (Alchemy Sort) · developer: desineart · updated October 9, 2026

The game works offline and does not require an account. Progress and settings stay on your device. Android versions with advertising offer optional rewarded ads to players aged 16 and over; younger players can play without advertising.

1. Local game data

The app stores levels, stars, coins, gems, unlocked items, story progress and preferences locally. On Android, it also stores a selected age category (under 16, 16–17, or 18+) separately from game progress. We do not request or store a birth date. The category is used only to decide whether advertising may start and whether teen protections apply. It is not sent to our servers; the advertising SDK receives the applicable age-treatment flag for older players. Reset progress removes game progress but preserves privacy preferences. Clearing app storage or uninstalling removes the local category and game data.

2. Optional advertising on Android

Android versions with advertising integrate Google AdMob (Google Mobile Ads SDK) and Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP). No ads or consent requests are started before an age category is selected. For players under 16, neither advertising nor consent collection is started and rewarded-ad buttons are hidden. For players aged 16–17, teen age treatment is applied. Ad content is restricted to Google's G rating for all players. The app removes Android Advertising ID and AdServices permissions. It does not use mediation networks.

For eligible players, UMP checks whether a consent message is required in their region before the advertising SDK is initialized. When required, privacy choices can be reopened from Settings. Depending on consent, Google may serve personalized, non-personalized or limited ads, or no ads. Rewarded ads are optional and initiated using a clearly labeled button showing the reward. Eligible players may also see banners and interstitial ads between completed levels. Closing an ad before Google confirms eligibility does not grant the reward.

3. Data processed by Google

When advertising is enabled for an eligible player, Google may process IP address (including approximate location derived from it), ad and app interactions, diagnostic information and device or account identifiers such as the app set ID. Android Advertising ID access is disabled by this app. Consent preferences may be stored and processed by UMP. These services use data for advertising, measurement, analytics, security and fraud prevention. Data is encrypted in transit. The developer does not operate an advertising-data server or receive a player's individual ad profile. Google controls its own retention and deletion practices.

Read Google's Privacy Policy, how Google uses data from partner apps, and AdMob's data disclosure.

4. Children

The game is available to children. Players who select under 16 can use the full game without advertising SDK initialization or consent requests. We do not request names, email addresses, precise location, contacts, photos or dates of birth. There is no chat, login, real-money purchase or separate analytics SDK. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children on our own servers. A parent can clear app storage or uninstall the app to remove local information.

5. Versions without advertising

Versions without an advertising integration, including the current iOS implementation, keep game data locally and do not start AdMob or UMP. An internet connection is not required for gameplay; on Android it is used for consent services, optional ads and opening external privacy links.

6. Your choices and contact

You can play without watching ads, reopen advertising privacy choices in Settings when available, or remove local data by clearing app storage or uninstalling. This app has no user account to delete. For privacy questions, open an issue at github.com/desineart/alchemysort-privacy/issues. Do not include personal or children's information in a public issue.

7. Changes

Updates will be published at this address with a revised date. Advertising choices are requested again when required by the consent service.
