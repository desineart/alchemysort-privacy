Privacy Policy

Water Sort: Alchemy Lab · developer: desineart · effective date: September 25, 2026

In short: the game does not collect, store on servers, share or sell any personal data. There are no accounts, no analytics and no tracking. Your progress stays on your device.

1. What the app stores

To save your progress the game keeps a small local record on your device: levels and stars, coins and gems, items you have unlocked, your settings (language, sound, music, colors) and story progress. This record never leaves your device and is not sent to us or anyone else. It is deleted when you remove the app, or with “Reset progress” in the settings.

2. What the app does not do

  • No registration, login or user accounts.
  • No collection of names, e‑mail addresses, phone numbers, contacts, photos, location or device identifiers.
  • No analytics, crash reporting or advertising SDKs in the current version.
  • No tracking across other apps or websites.
  • No in‑app purchases with real money. Coins and gems are earned in the game only.
  • No internet connection is required to play.

3. Children

The game is suitable for all ages. Because we collect no personal data, we do not knowingly collect any data from children.

4. Advertising in future versions

A later version may add optional rewarded ads through a third‑party ad network. Before that happens this policy will be updated to name the network and describe what data it processes, and, where the law requires it, the game will ask for your consent first.

5. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be published at this address with a new effective date.

6. Contact

Questions about this policy: open an issue at github.com/desineart/alchemysort-privacy/issues.
